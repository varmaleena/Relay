# Official criteria and Relay assessment

Official themes/criteria and FAQ rechecked on 17 October 2026. Terms were previously checked on 14 October; private dashboard eligibility/cutoff remains unverified. Sources are official, not dossier assertions or third-party summaries.

Official criteria: technical merit/GenAI implementation 40%, problem alignment/impact 25%, innovation/creativity 25%, UX/design 10%. Relay fits the BFSI fraud-risk theme. Required submission artifacts are a functioning Google Cloud deployed URL, public GitHub repository, video strictly under three minutes and explanatory deck. English submission materials are required. Teams contain 2–4 eligible professionals/entrepreneurs/startup members, all 21+ and resident in JAPAC; students are ineligible. Formation closes October 14; submission October 18. Exact dashboard cutoff remains unverified.
