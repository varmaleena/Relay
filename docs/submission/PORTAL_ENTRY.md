# Relay submission text

## Title

Relay: guided scam-response reports

## Theme

BFSI: Intelligent Risk, Fraud & Financial Experiences

## Description

Relay helps someone who has just experienced a scam organize an accurate account,
review a proposed contact order and prepare recipient-specific reports for
self-submission. The person confirms key facts and controls the final report.
Copying or downloading a report does not file a complaint.

Gemini through the Google GenAI SDK and ADK workflows supports intake,
classification and drafting. Deterministic domain checks, source references,
version proofs and model verification keep reports reviewable. Firebase Hosting,
Cloud Run, Firestore, private Cloud Storage and Pub/Sub provide the cloud workflow;
Firebase authentication, App Check and owner checks protect case access.

The prototype demonstrates synthetic data only. Bank, police and payment actions
are explicitly simulated, and source guidance remains unreviewed draft material.
Voice automatic fact saving, Studio/linking cloud integration, human language/
source reviews and comparative quality evaluations retain open gates. We do not
claim guaranteed recovery, official acceptance or an achieved general reliability
target. The submitted video shows the working text-first journey.
